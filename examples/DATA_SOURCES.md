# Attribution and data rights for online cart research examples

The running product search uses **Open Products Facts**:
https://world.openproductsfacts.org/
and its product search API at
https://world.openproductsfacts.org/cgi/search.pl

The optional historical price lookup uses **Open Prices**:
https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/

Public product data is community-contributed and should be attributed to its
source. Open Products Facts operates within the Open Food Facts ecosystem
whose product database is distributed under the Open Database License (ODbL).
Please verify applicable licensing before redistributing aggregate datasets:
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use
and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/

The **MIT license in LICENSE covers original TraceCart code, not third-party
product databases, retailer content or consumer reviews**. Any archived
live product snapshot below includes source URL, retrieval date and a warning
that it is a historical observation. Source records are not user-generated
test results and are not presented as confirmed retail offers.

Do not publish personal purchase history, buyer IDs, private checkout tokens
or unrelated cart data.
